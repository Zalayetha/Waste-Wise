# Food waste logging

A shift guide for sorting, weighing, and recording discarded food.

## 01 Waste categories

**Prep scraps.** Food discarded during preparation, such as vegetable peels.

**Unsold food.** Food prepared for sale that was never served and is discarded.

**Customer leftovers.** Food returned on a plate or tray after service.

## 02 How to log waste

Record the date, food item, waste category, and net weight in kilograms (kg). Use a separate entry for each food item and category. If your scale shows grams, divide by 1,000 before entering the weight.

**Example entry.** 7 November 2026 | Tomato trimmings | Prep scraps | 0.25 kg

## 03 Special cases

**Donated food.** Record donated items in the donation log. If you donate some of an unsold batch and discard the rest, enter only the discarded portion in the waste log.

**Weighing with a container.** Tare the empty container before adding food. If you already weighed both together, subtract the empty container weight. A filled bin weighing 2.4 kg with an empty weight of 0.6 kg contains 1.8 kg of food waste.
